Community

In the heart of the village

Woodland, Wildlife and Wellbeing Garden

The land between the brook and the church is becoming a community garden for everyone. A new path, funded and built by Parr Bridge Retail Park, now links Church Vale through the church grounds to Mosley Common Road, the shops and the school.

There are plenty of ideas for what comes next. They depend on funding and on volunteers.

Read our vision statement

As Christians we are called by Christ first and foremost to be wholeheartedly committed to Him as our Saviour and Lord, and called together to be part of His people, the church. As a local church we aim to be committed to:

  1. worshipping the living Lord regularly, in a way that honours God, reflects the community and attracts newcomers;
  2. praying for God's work in the parish, the nation and across His world, and for one another;
  3. sensitively sharing the good news of Jesus with those around us, wherever He has placed us;
  4. allowing the Holy Spirit to help us grow in Christian maturity and form the character of Jesus within us;
  5. seeking out the gifts of every church member and encouraging their use in the service of Christ;
  6. encouraging and supporting every church member in every practical way, building up the church in love;
  7. our community, showing God's love by our concern for those who suffer and our search for justice and reconciliation;
  8. actively supporting those involved in mission work, at home and abroad.
